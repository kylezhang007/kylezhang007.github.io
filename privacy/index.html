Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

This policy covers the Google OAuth client "rclone" registered by Kyle Zhang.

  • Who uses it: only the owner (Kyle Zhang), to back up his own files. It is not distributed to or used by anyone else.
  • What it accesses: only files in the owner's Google Drive that this client itself created (scope drive.file). It cannot see other files.
  • Where data goes: files move only between the owner's own computer and the owner's own Google Drive. No data is sent to any third party, sold, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Storage of credentials: the OAuth token is stored only on the owner's computer in the rclone configuration file.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
